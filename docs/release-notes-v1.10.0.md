# APEX CW v1.10.0

Includes everything of 1.9.0, which never reached the Play Store (the last store version is 1.8.0). A set of all prosigns, the original Koch sequences, shortcuts on the app icon
and more control for the audio trainer on the lock screen.

## New
- **Prosigns character set:** a new top-level set next to Koch, All characters
  and Practice set with all ten prosigns (AS KA KN SK VE BK AR BT HH SOS). In
  the typing modes the on-screen keyboard gets a row of prosign keys (#156).
- **Prosigns everywhere:** the character chart, the Morse tree and the audio
  trainer now list all ten prosigns; the audio trainer speaks the meanings of
  AR, BT, HH and SOS in both voices.
- **Audio trainer:** speaks punctuation and prosigns, with a choice of how a
  prosign is introduced (word, bell, harp or gong); tempo and character
  spacing buttons in the lock-screen and notification controls (#152); solution
  coloured by character type and following the upper/lower-case setting.
- **App shortcuts:** long-press the launcher icon to open Listen, Send, Audio
  trainer and more directly; choose and order them in Settings > Appearance (#151).
- **Progress view:** effective WPM as a KPI tile and a second curve.

## Changed
- **Koch sequences:** LCWO, CW Academy and LICW now follow the original lists
  of the courses, without extra prosigns. The Morserino sequence keeps the
  firmware's list and is renamed from "M32"; it stands between LICW and Custom.
- **Q-groups:** only the tempo slider is left. A group is heard as one melody
  and one is played per question, so character and word spacing (and "eff.")
  are gone.
- The audio trainer no longer shows the hint about umlauts and CH.

## From 1.9.0 (never in the store)
- **Audio trainer (Free modes):** you hear a group in Morse, think the
  characters, then hear them spelled out. Own Koch level and character set,
  two voices, interference button. It keeps running with the screen off, with
  notification, lock-screen and headset controls.
- **Confirmation tone:** Classic, Marimba, Gentle or none in Settings > General (#136).
- **Heat map:** explains what a column is.
- **Fixed:** crash when pressing Stop in the background.
