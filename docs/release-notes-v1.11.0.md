# APEX CW v1.11.0

Training goals and a new timing analysis for the straight key.

## New
- **Training goals:** each training has an ordered list of goals (new
  characters, character and word spacing, group length, speed). After a block
  the app suggests one step at a time: Apply, Not now (15 minutes) or undo.
  The "On the way to" card shows a goal bar and a readiness bar with a status
  word; tap it for the numbers. Weak characters and mix-ups share one card.
  Thresholds are kept per training; the goal list sits in each settings sheet.
- **Straight-key timing (USB adapter, e.g. VBand):**
  - **CW Keyer:** a "Rhythm" panel shows how even your dits, dahs and gaps are
    (switch it in Settings > Keyer, "Timing analysis in the CW Keyer") (#164).
  - **Send result page:** a "Timing" card with an index from 0 to 100 and a
    breakdown by dit, dah, element gap, letter gap and word gap (#166).
  - **Send progress tab:** the index or one median ratio per block over time,
    optionally for one speed band (#167).

## Changed
- Goal settings: descriptions left-aligned, clearer texts.
- Audio trainer: the solution is coloured by character type; the umlaut hint
  is gone.
- The old adaptive-mode suggestions are replaced by the training goals.
